House rules

  • No client names. Titles, text and photographs name the type and the location, never the client.
  • Numbers as IAQ records them. Area, class and programme length are copied, not rounded.
  • Leave it empty rather than estimate. A missing field shows as a gap; an estimate shows as a fact.
  • One idea per sentence. Challenge, approach and result are two or three sentences each.
  • Photographs cleared for public use. No client signs, logos or client staff without consent.
  • Values, dates and quotes need written clearance. Contract values and client quotes appear only when cleared.
