The walk-through rules (43)

The walk-through rules

Every rule set during the walk-through, numbered so a reasoning cell can cite it. They hold for every page.

  1. 1. A description that runs to two lines is balanced: two lines of near-equal length, never one long and one short. Home hero, 22 Oct
  2. 2. Air between a description and its button: the button sits a clear step below the text. Home hero, 22 Oct
  3. 3. A title must mean something a reader can take away. Not a label of what the block is (in numbers) but a statement the block proves. Home numbers block, 22 Oct
  4. 4. Figures and their icons are not a list: display-size numbers, a mark beside each, a label under, so the record reads as proof. Home numbers block, 22 Oct
  5. 5. A stage readout shows the stage number only, never 3 / 6. Home delivery cycle, 22 Oct
  6. 6. Every panel is part of the page: a card with the page's own button and a way to continue (next, previous), never a floating node with a text link. Home delivery cycle, 22 Oct
  7. 7. Icons are the isometric set. The older photoreal and line marks are replaced wherever they still stand. Home delivery cycle, 22 Oct
  8. 8. No full stop at the end of a title. Commas are fine, they carry meaning. Home markets band, 22 Oct; applied to every title on the site
  9. 9. A line break falls where a thought ends. A description that is split in two tones breaks before the second thought, never with its first word dangling. Home markets band, 22 Oct
  10. 10. No repeated information in one view. Repeated design is fine, it is consistency; repeated content is not. Services menu, 22 Oct
  11. 11. Menu hover: the word goes a touch heavier and red. No dot, no marker. Navigation, 22 Oct
  12. 12. A dropdown has no coloured outline; a neutral hairline and a shadow. Navigation, 22 Oct
  13. 13. Every button and icon answers the pointer: colour, a one-pixel lift, a darker button. Navigation, 22 Oct
  14. 14. Type sizes: body text on Tony Robbins' site measures 20 px, nav 16 px. IAQ runs 15 px body and 18.5 px ledes at a 1.12 zoom (about 17 and 21 px on screen). Decision pending: a body step up to 16 or 17 px. 22 Oct
  15. 15. An inner page banner is not the home banner: about half the window, never the full height. About, 22 Oct
  16. 16. No white fade or blur on a banner. The picture ends where the section ends. About, 22 Oct
  17. 17. On every page that opens on a dark banner the bar runs clear at the top and goes solid on scroll, as on the home page. About, 22 Oct
  18. 18. A proof block (certificates, awards) is centred: title, the marks, the line and the link in the middle, with room above and below. About achievements, 22 Oct
  19. 19. The member area is designed in every state: signed out, wrong passcode, signed in, the portal, the gate, signed out again. Actions in sentence case. Member area, 22 Oct
  20. 20. A small-caps label keeps its style but not its tracking: letter-spacing.04em at most, site wide. Wide tracking reads as scattered. Footer label, 22 Oct
  21. 21. The orphan wrap: a caption, a label or a one-sentence line never breaks into a second line of one or two words. It runs the full width of what it sits under, or it is one line. History captions, 22 Oct
  22. 22. A timeline shows the flow of time: an axis that draws as it is read, the passed marks filled, the live one lit, the year at reading size. Unless the layout is responsive and there really is no room (a phone), where a short last line is allowed. History, 22 Oct
  23. 23. No standalone illustration where a photograph of IAQ's own work exists. History, 22 Oct
  24. 24. A breadcrumb sits at the top of the banner, under the bar, never at its foot above the title. History banner, 22 Oct
  25. 25. A figure that is the point of the page (the years) is set at display size, not as a small label. History banner, 22 Oct
  26. 26. Say a thing once per page: a hero line is not repeated as a foot heading. Where the foot needs a heading, give it the figure. History, 22 Oct
  27. 27. & in a title only where it joins two names (Engineering, Procurement & Construction; Food & Beverage); "and" inside a sentence title. 22 Oct
  28. 28. A two-part title breaks before its red phrase: the statement on one line, the phrase on the next, never mid-phrase. The same for the description under it: balanced lines. Commitment, 22 Oct
  29. 29. A banner's air is balanced: the same room above the title as below the last row. A scroll cue sits on the controls row, at the left, never on a line of its own. Services map, 22 Oct
  30. 30. A menu column does not change on hover: no when-asked tags, no lighting and dimming. The hover is the row under the pointer, nothing else. Services menu, 22 Oct
  31. 31. A hover rule (the red line that draws on hover) runs along the FOOT of a card or tile, everywhere on the site. Markets grid, 22 Oct
  32. 32. A picture tile rests a little grey and comes to full colour under the pointer. No zoom on hover. Markets grid, 22 Oct
  33. 33. Tiles in a set are equal. No outsized first tile. Markets grid, 22 Oct
  34. 34. An arrow stands alone: no circle behind it, no box behind an icon, no square within a square. Markets grid, PCU & TTI, 22 Oct
  35. 35. Nothing that cannot be clicked reacts to the pointer: no lift, no light, no rule on a card that is not a link. A card that animates on its own needs no hover. PCU & TTI reasons, 22 Oct
  36. 36. No icon in a circle, no dashed ring, no dot-dot-dot decoration. Where an icon is thin, use the more detailed drawing. PCU & TTI delivery approach, 22 Oct
  37. 37. The Services facility map: the front face must read clean. Done 23 Oct: the cladding joints fade with distance and go out on a pick; the picked state keeps its depth and tints only what is hidden; the building's skin never ghosts. Services map, 22 Oct; done 23 Oct
  38. 38. No eyebrow above a title unless it carries a fact. Start a conversation, Where we build and their kind are off. EFM band, 22 Oct
  39. 39. A call-to-action band stacks: the title and the line on top, the button anchored bottom right, the phone at the left of the same row. The same on every page. EFM band, 22 Oct
  40. 40. Every page is swept against these rules, not only the ones walked. Each change is recorded here so a design can be vetoed. 22 Oct
  41. 41. The 3D and animation get the same polish: timing, labels, the view at rest. Done 23 Oct on the district cooling tour (labels after the camera, the tank on the step's clock, stations named once, a dwell per step with a time bar, the tour ends on the overview) and on the Services map (rule 37). 22 Oct; done 23 Oct
  42. 42. Never a design that has no function: no ring, circle, line or dot put in to fill space. Density over white space: the client does not like white space; negative space only where it does a job. Market capability cards, 22 Oct
  43. 43. On a phone a banner's headline, line and button stay together; no dead gap pushing the line to the foot. Home on the phone, 22 Oct
How this page is made

How this works

Every page, cut into its sections. Before is the section exactly as the 22 Oct build has it, captured at 1440 wide. After appears only once that section changed. Reasoning says why the copy changed and why the design changed, one line each.

We go through it one by one. A section with no After is still open. Notes typed in the reasoning cell stay in this browser; Save file bakes them into the page.

The first pass is the order of the first review: home, the three unit pages, the markets. The rest of the site follows in site order.
