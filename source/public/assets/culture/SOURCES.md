# Culture and Contact photographs: sources (15 Sep 2026)

Every file in this folder is a photograph IAQ published itself, on its newsroom (iaqtechnology.com.my) or its
LinkedIn company page (linkedin.com/company/iaq-group-of-companies). None is stock, Google Maps, Street View or
third party press. Each was looked at before use and is captioned on the page for what it shows.

Converted to WebP, max 1600 px wide, quality 0.8, with `tools/webp-culture-0915.mjs` (headless Chrome canvas
encoder). Two files are cropped, as noted. Full research notes: session scratchpad `research/iaq-culture-sources.md`.

Photographs show identifiable staff. Clear the selection with IAQ HR before launch.

## Correction on record

`/assets/photo-opening.webp` is the IMS internal audit opening meeting in an IAQ boardroom (IAQ newsroom, 8 May 2025,
https://iaqtechnology.com.my/excellence-through-integration-iaq-reinforces-commitment-to-ims-and-global-standards/,
original 2025/06/Opening.png). The post does not say which office. It used to be captioned as the Penang office on
Culture and used as the Penang representation on Contact. Both pages now use the real Penang opening photograph,
and the strip captions photo-opening.webp as the audit meeting.
