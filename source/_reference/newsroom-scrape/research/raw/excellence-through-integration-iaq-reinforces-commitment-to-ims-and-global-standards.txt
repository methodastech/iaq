Excellence Through Integration: IAQ Reinforces Commitment to IMS and Global Standards
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / May 8, 2025 
At IAQ, quality, safety, and environmental responsibility are not just aspirations—they are embedded in every facet of our operations. Through our Integrated Management System (IMS), we uphold world-class standards that ensure our projects meet the rigorous demands of the high-tech industries we serve.
As a fully certified partner, IAQ proudly adheres to the following internationally recognized standards:
ISO 9001:2015 – Quality Management Systems
ISO 14001:2015 – Environmental Management Systems
ISO 45001:2018 – Occupational Health & Safety Management Systems
Driving Operational Reliability Through Internal Audits
In a recent internal audit, IAQ once again demonstrated our steadfast compliance with all three standards. This audit not only confirmed the robustness of our systems, but also reaffirmed our commitment to continuous improvement across the organization. These efforts are essential to our promise of delivering high-precision, high-performance facility solutions.
Why Our IMS Matters to Our Clients
Our integrated approach brings meaningful advantages to every stakeholder:
Consistent Quality across all phases of project execution
Risk Mitigation through uniform, standardized procedures
Environmental Responsibility embedded into operational practices
Workplace Safety maintained through proactive, certified protocols
Culture of Continuous Improvement driving long-term value
A Team Effort in Pursuit of Excellence
We extend our appreciation to the internal audit team and every department involved in this essential exercise. Your diligence and professionalism continue to strengthen IAQ’s position as a trusted partner for engineered facility solutions.
At IAQ, these certifications are more than just badges—they are a living commitment to our clients. Through IMS, we deliver projects that stand for excellence, sustainability, and safety—every single time.
