IAQ Annual Dinner 2025: Hawaiian Night Getaway Strengthens Team Spirit and Togetherness
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / May 2, 2025 
From April 25th to 27th, IAQ brought the team together for an unforgettable retreat at the stunning DoubleTree by Hilton Damai Laut Resort. This year’s Annual Dinner 2025 embraced a lively “Hawaiian Night” theme—blending celebration, reflection, and team bonding into one meaningful experience.
Energising the Team with Purpose
The three-day, two-night getaway began with a dynamic team-building session designed to foster collaboration, trust, and unity across departments. Through a series of games and challenges, team members worked hand-in-hand to strengthen connections, proving that behind every successful project is a foundation of mutual support and shared goals.
Hawaiian Night: A Celebration of Culture and Camaraderie
The pinnacle of the retreat was the Hawaiian-themed Annual Dinner, where IAQ’s signature spirit of fun and family came to life. The evening unfolded with tropical décor, a sumptuous spread of food, and captivating performances. The lucky draw segment kept the energy high, culminating in a surprise grand prize announcement by IAQ’s CEO—an exciting twist that brought cheers and smiles across the ballroom.
A Grateful Reflection
The retreat was a powerful reminder of what makes IAQ exceptional—not just our projects or capabilities, but our people. From the organizing committee to every employee who brought their enthusiasm and heart, the event was a shared success that underscored the strength of our culture.
At IAQ, we believe that building a strong team is just as important as building world-class facilities. As we look to the future, this celebration of unity and gratitude fuels our drive to reach new milestones—together.
