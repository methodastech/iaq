IAQ Showcases Cleanroom Expertise at ENGINEER & MARVEX 2024
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / September 26, 2024 
IAQ was honored to participate in ENGINEER & MARVEX 2024, ASEAN’s premier engineering and ACMV&R exhibition and conference, held at the Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre. The event brought together industry leaders, professionals, and innovators for a dynamic exchange of ideas, networking opportunities, and business development.
As a company committed to advancing high-tech facility solutions, IAQ was delighted to engage with peers and explore emerging technologies that are shaping the future of engineering and environmental control systems in the region.
A highlight of our participation was a presentation delivered by our esteemed colleague, Ang Soo Leng, who took the stage to share valuable insights on cleanroom solutions with a distinguished audience. His presentation reinforced IAQ’s position as a thought leader in the cleanroom industry and underscored our dedication to knowledge-sharing and innovation.
Our involvement in ENGINEER & MARVEX 2024 reflects our ongoing mission to support the development of sophisticated, future-ready facilities across Southeast Asia. As a specialist in cleanroom solutions, IAQ is proud to contribute to the progress of critical industries such as semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, and electronics manufacturing.
We look forward to continuing our journey of excellence and innovation, delivering world-class solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients and partners.
