IAQ Featured in The Star: A National Spotlight on Excellence in Hi-Tech Facility Construction
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / December 24, 2024 
IAQ is honored to be featured in The Star following our recent recognition as the Builder of the Year at the Malaysian Construction Industry Excellence Awards (MCIEA) 2024, presented by the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) Malaysia.
This prestigious spotlight underscores our growing influence in the development of high-tech facilities—including cleanrooms, wafer fabrication plants, and data centres—where precision, performance, and innovation are paramount.
Industry-Wide Recognition
“Together, we are shaping the future of hi-tech facilities,” shared Ir. Tiew Soon Aik, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of IAQ Solutions.
To be acknowledged by a respected national publication and honored by CIDB is a milestone that reflects more than just our technical capability—it celebrates the collective efforts of our stakeholders, partners, subcontractors, vendors, and the dedicated IAQ team. Each contribution has played a vital role in helping us set new benchmarks for quality, safety, and performance across our projects.
The feature in The Star highlights not only our recent accomplishments but also our long-term vision: to lead Malaysia’s transformation into a regional hub for advanced, sustainable, and digitally-integrated infrastructure in the high-tech sector.
