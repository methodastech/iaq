IAQ Engages Future Engineers at UTAR’s Engineering Science and Fiesta: The Return 2025
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / March 5, 2025 
IAQ was proud to participate in Engineering Science and Fiesta: The Return 2025, a premier event hosted by Universiti Tunku Abdul Rahman (UTAR) that celebrates innovation, knowledge sharing, and community engagement in the fields of engineering and science.
This vibrant event provided a valuable platform for industry and academia to come together, fostering meaningful connections between engineering professionals and the next generation of talent. IAQ took the opportunity to introduce our company’s mission, capabilities, and contributions to Malaysia’s high-tech facility construction sector to UTAR’s aspiring engineers.
Through direct engagement and open dialogue, our team shared real-world insights on what it takes to succeed in a fast-evolving engineering landscape—highlighting the importance of continuous learning, cross-disciplinary collaboration, and a strong commitment to safety and innovation.
“We believe these students represent the future of our industry,” said a representative from IAQ. “Their passion and curiosity are inspiring, and we’re confident that many will go on to make significant contributions in the field.”
IAQ extends sincere thanks to UTAR for the invitation and looks forward to more opportunities to support young talent through industry-academic collaboration.
Together, we’re shaping a smarter, stronger future for engineering in Malaysia.
