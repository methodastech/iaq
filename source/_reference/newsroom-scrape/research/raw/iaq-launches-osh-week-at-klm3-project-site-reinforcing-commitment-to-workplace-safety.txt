IAQ Launches OSH Week at KLM3 Project Site, Reinforcing Commitment to Workplace Safety
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / July 31, 2024 
IAQ is proud to be part of the official launch of Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) Week at our Northern Project—a dedicated initiative focused on reinforcing our unwavering commitment to creating a safer, healthier, and more ethical working environment.
OSH Week serves as a vital platform to strengthen the culture of safety and promote collective responsibility across all levels of the project team. Through a series of engaging programs and awareness activities, the initiative aims to instill a strong safety mindset, encourage proactive risk management, and uphold the highest standards of occupational health practices.
The week-long event underscores IAQ’s dedication not only to regulatory compliance but to cultivating an environment where every worker feels responsible for maintaining safety—both for themselves and their colleagues.
Adding to the significance of this milestone, we are honored to share that IAQ has received a Certificate of Appreciation from our valued client. This recognition celebrates the collaborative efforts and dedication demonstrated by the IAQ team in upholding safety excellence throughout the project lifecycle.
As OSH Week unfolds, we invite all team members and stakeholders to actively participate and make it a meaningful and impactful experience. Together, let us continue to foster a culture of safety and well-being—because every safe step we take today builds the foundation for a stronger tomorrow.
