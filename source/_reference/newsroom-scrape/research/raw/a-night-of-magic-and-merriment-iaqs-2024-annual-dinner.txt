A Night of Magic and Merriment: IAQ’s 2024 Annual Dinner
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / April 24, 2024 
The IAQ 2024 Annual Dinner at the Shangri-La Golden Sands Resort in Penang was not just a celebration, but a spectacle to behold! 
Our Cosplay Night-themed Annual Dinner was an unforgettable extravaganza, filled with laughter, camaraderie, and a dash of fantasy. A heartfelt salute to our incredible IAQ team members who embraced the spirit of cosplay, morphing into beloved characters from the realms of film, anime, and television. The night was a testament to the vibrant creativity and youthful spirit that thrives within our team.
The evening was a grand affair, brimming with delectable cuisine, enchanting live music, and a plethora of exciting prizes. A standing ovation for our lucky draw winners and a special salute to the recipients of our long-service and appreciation awards. We are privileged to collaborate with such a gifted and diligent team.
We extend our deepest gratitude to our vendors for their generous sponsorships, which added an extra sparkle to this memorable event. Here’s to creating more magical memories with our IAQ family!
