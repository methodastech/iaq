IAQ Supports Kempen Tolak Gula Campaign to Promote Workplace Health
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / October 17, 2024 
At IAQ, we believe that a healthy workforce is the foundation of a thriving and high-performing organization. In line with this commitment, our team recently participated in the Kempen Tolak Gula (Say No to Sugar Campaign), an initiative organized by PERKESO to raise awareness about the dangers of excessive sugar consumption.
This important campaign aims to educate employees on the long-term health risks associated with high sugar intake, including diabetes, obesity, and cardiovascular diseases. By promoting healthier lifestyle choices, the initiative supports the creation of more resilient, energetic, and informed workplaces.
At IAQ, employee well-being is a top priority. Our participation in the campaign reflects our proactive approach to corporate health and wellness, where we strive to build a work environment that encourages better habits, increased health literacy, and long-term preventive care.
We extend our sincere thanks to PERKESO for leading this impactful effort. Together, we are taking meaningful steps to create a healthier workplace and reduce the risk of chronic diseases that can affect productivity and quality of life.
Let’s continue to make health a shared responsibility—because a healthier team is a stronger team.
