Reflecting on 2023: A Message from IAQ Group CEO
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / January 15, 2024 
As we draw the curtains on 2023, I am honored to reflect on IAQ Group’s exceptional journey this year. As Your Total Facility Solutions Provider, serving high-tech facilities across industrial sectors, our dedication to excellence has been unwavering. One of our pivotal accomplishments has been the expansion of our footprint through groundbreaking new projects. We’ve embarked on ventures that pushes our technical prowess to new height, cementing our commitment to revolutionizing hi-tech industries with innovative solutions.
Moreover, I am incredibly proud to announce IAQ Group’s attainment of ISO 14001 & ISO 45001 Certification this year, a testament to our unwavering dedication to quality and adherence to international standards. Our pledge to Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles has been at the forefront of our operations. Sustainability driving us to create environmentally conscious, socially responsible, and ethically sound solutions.
None of this would have been possible without the incredible dedication and hard work of our remarkable team. To our staff, stakeholders, clients, suppliers, and subcontractors: your unwavering support and commitment have been the cornerstone of our success. Together, we’ve navigated challenges, celebrated victories, and built lasting relationships that form the very fabric of IAQ’s success.
As we step into 2024, let’s carry forward the spirit of collaboration and innovation that defines us. I express my deepest gratitude to everyone who has contributed to our journey. Here’s to another year of growth, impact, and shared accomplishments!
With appreciation,
Ir. Tiew Soon Aik
Chief Executive Officer
