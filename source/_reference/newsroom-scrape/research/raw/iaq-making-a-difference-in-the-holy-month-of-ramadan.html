 IAQ Making a Difference in the Holy Month of Ramadan

Leave a Comment / By Nabilahhradzi / April 3, 2024

As the sacred month of Ramadan envelops us in its blessings, IAQ is honored to continue our tradition of giving back to the community. This year, IAQ reaching out to those who need it the most: the orphans and HIV+ children under the special care of PAKATS home.
 
IAQ organized a heartwarming iftar program for 16 children at PAKATS home. These young souls, who deserve love and care, fostering bonds of compassion and unity.
 
In the spirit of Hari Raya, IAQ extended our support by donating brand-new clothes to these children. Our hope is that these garments will not only adorn their bodies but also fill their hearts with joy during this festive season.
 
IAQ invite our dedicated staff to be part of this meaningful cause. Your involvement can create ripples of kindness, touching lives and brightening futures.
