IAQ Joins Prime Minister’s High-Level Business Roundtable in France, Representing Malaysia’s Growing Global Presence
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / July 9, 2025 
IAQ is honored to have been selected by MATRADE to participate in a high-level business roundtable with Prime Minister Dato’ Seri Anwar Ibrahim during his official visit to France, a distinction that underscores our standing as a recognized contributor to Malaysia’s international business agenda.
The roundtable brought together over 40 French industry leaders alongside Malaysian companies, as part of a broader gathering of more than 180 organizations convened to deepen Malaysia-France economic ties. Discussions spanned strategic opportunities across technology, economic development, and education sectors that are central to both nations’ long-term growth ambitions. The meeting also builds on the strong bilateral trade relationship between Malaysia and France, valued at RM50.91 billion.
For IAQ, this invitation is more than a moment of recognition. It is an affirmation of the trust we have built with our clients, our partners, and the broader industry and a signal of the role we intend to play in Malaysia’s expanding global footprint.
We look forward to translating the connections forged in France into meaningful partnerships that create tangible value for our stakeholders and contribute to Malaysia’s continued prominence on the world stage.
