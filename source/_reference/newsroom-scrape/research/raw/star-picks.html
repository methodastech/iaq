    JOINT EFFORTS IN OVERCOMING PAIN

IAQ Solutions Sdn Bhd, a design and build turnkey contractor, has set a new benchmark in the hi-tech facility industry.

The company recently cemented its position as a leader in delivering total facility solutions after being crowned as Builder of the Year at the prestigious Malaysian Construction Industry Excellence Awards (MCIEA) 2024 by the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB).

This achievement not only reflects the innovation and quality embedded in IAQ’s operations but also the dedication of its founder and chief executive officer Ir. Tiew Soon Aik, whose leadership continues to drive the company to greater heights.

Building global excellence

With a global footprint spanning seven countries, including Malaysia, Singapore, China, Sweden, France, Poland and Morocco, IAQ has established itself as a trusted partner in cleanroom solutions.

The company specialises in delivering tailored, cutting-edge facility solutions for hi-tech industries, ensuring a seamless balance of precision, efficiency and environmental sustainability.

A tribute to teamwork and collaboration

IAQ has consistently upheld its reputation as a trailblazer in the industry, offering world-class services tailored to meet the needs of hi-tech facilities globally.

Its dedication to excellence has earned the company recognition as a total facility solutions provider, setting it apart from competitors and driving trust within the global market.

At the heart of IAQ’s success lies its unwavering commitment to teamwork and collaboration.

A special acknowledgement goes out to its dedicated staff, subcontractors and vendors whose contributions were instrumental in achieving this milestone.

The company extends its deepest gratitude to its trusted suppliers, including Nanjing TICA Climate Solutions Co., Ltd, Passive Fire Protection Sdn Bhd, Japan Air Filter Asia Sdn Bhd, Channel Systems Asia, Ricwil (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd, Welcome Air-Tech Sales & Services Sdn Bhd, Green Excel Engineering and Consultancy Sdn Bhd, Truwater Cooling Towers Sdn Bhd, Smardt Malaysia Sdn Bhd, Ruskin (M) Sdn Bhd, Mayair Manufacturing (M) Sdn Bhd, Insafoam Insulation (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd and Envalco Sdn Bhd.

IAQ also appreciates the exceptional work of its sub-contractors, who played a pivotal role in this achievement, such as Clanicon Builders Sdn Bhd, Sin Sentech Aluminium Glass Sdn Bhd, GNH Engineering Sdn Bhd, SPK Asas Sdn Bhd, KEESS Global Pte Ltd, Shun Yi Cheng Contract Engineering Pte Ltd, Sinetek Engineering Sdn Bhd, Adept Construction and Engineering Sdn Bhd, LCE M&E Engineering Sdn Bhd, Nexon Energy Sdn Bhd, KCLA Architects + Engineers, Liwo Bina Sdn Bhd and Pan Engineering (North) Sdn Bhd.

The road ahead

As IAQ celebrates its momentous achievement, the company remains steadfast in its mission to deliver excellence.

Guided by strong leadership and a commitment to innovation, IAQ continues to redefine standards in the cleanroom and hi-tech facility industry.

“Thank you to our dedicated staff, sub-contractors and vendors for their unwavering dedication and contributions to our success. Together, we are shaping the future of hi-tech facilities,” expressed Tiew.

For more insights into IAQ Solutions and its industry-leading services, visit iaqtechnology.com.my.
