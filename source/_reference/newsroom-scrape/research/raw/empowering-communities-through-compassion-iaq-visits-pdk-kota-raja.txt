Empowering Communities Through Compassion: IAQ Visits PDK Kota Raja
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / April 10, 2025 
As part of its ongoing Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives, IAQ recently extended a helping hand to Pusat Pemulihan Dalam Komuniti (PDK) Kota Raja in Klang, reaffirming its commitment to making a meaningful difference in the lives of others.
During the visit, IAQ donated laptops, air-conditioning units, and gym mats—resources aimed at enhancing both the comfort and operational efficiency of the centre. These contributions are intended to support the dedicated caregivers, educators, and volunteers at PDK as they continue their vital work with children with special needs.
The IAQ team was warmly welcomed by the centre’s staff and volunteers, who shared touching stories and insights into their work with children affected by conditions such as autism and Down syndrome. Their passion, resilience, and unwavering dedication left a lasting impression and deepened the team’s appreciation for the challenges and rewards of such important community service.
“It was truly a humbling and eye-opening experience,” said a representative from IAQ. “The work being done at PDK Kota Raja is nothing short of inspiring, and we are honoured to play a small part in supporting their mission.”
IAQ remains committed to initiatives that go beyond business—focusing on community engagement, social awareness, and long-term impact. The company will continue to explore ways to uplift those in need, believing that real success is measured not only by corporate milestones, but also by the positive change we bring to the world around us.
