IAQ Solutions Shines at Penang’s Cultural Night Run
Last Saturday, our dedicated IAQ team in Penang proudly participated in the Cultural Night Run at the Penang Second Bridge. This extraordinary event was enlivened by a series of cultural performances along the route, including captivating Indian dance, vibrant Boria, and energetic Bhangra, creating an unforgettable experience for all participants. 
Our enthusiastic staff showcased their spirit and stamina across various categories: the Leisure Run (5KM), Cultural Run (12KM), and Competitive Run (21KM). This event was a fantastic opportunity to highlight our team’s camaraderie and unity. 
At IAQ, we believe in promoting a healthy lifestyle among our team members. Participating in events like this reinforces our commitment to well-being and fitness. Let’s keep the momentum going—keep running and stay healthy!
A heartfelt thank you to everyone who participated and made the night memorable for the entire IAQ family. We look forward to even greater participation in future events!
