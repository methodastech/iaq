IAQ Opens Official Branch Office in Penang, Strengthening Commitment to the Northern Region
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / July 23, 2025 
IAQ is pleased to announce the official opening of our Penang branch office. A milestone that marks the natural progression of our longstanding presence and client relationships in the northern region of Malaysia.
After years of supporting clients across Penang, establishing a dedicated office here reflects our commitment to being closer to the people and industries we serve. It is a step that allows us to respond with greater speed, build deeper partnerships, and deliver the standard of service our clients in the region deserve.
We are grateful to all clients, consultants, and stakeholders who joined us at the Grand Opening to mark this occasion. Your presence was a meaningful testament to the relationships we have built together over the years.
Our Penang office is now open and ready to welcome visitors. Whether you are an existing client, a prospective partner, or simply curious about what IAQ does, we invite you to drop by. To schedule a visit or enquire about our services, please reach out to us at info@iaqtechnology.com.my.
We look forward to deepening our roots in Penang and continuing to grow alongside the communities and industries we serve here.
