Celebrating Unity and Festivity: IAQ Eid Celebration 2025
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / April 16, 2025 
In the spirit of togetherness and celebration, IAQ hosted a vibrant Jamuan Raya to commemorate Hari Raya Aidilfitri with its employees at the headquarters. The celebration brought colleagues together for an afternoon filled with warmth, laughter, and tradition, reflecting the company’s people-first culture and appreciation for diversity.
Staff members embraced the festive spirit by donning colourful traditional attire—baju melayu and baju kurung—turning the workplace into a lively scene of cultural pride and joy. The sounds of cheerful greetings and heartfelt reunions echoed through the office, creating an atmosphere of unity and gratitude.
A highlight of the gathering was the indulgent spread of classic Raya delicacies, including lemang, rendang, satay, and an assortment of traditional kuih-muih. The carefully curated menu offered a taste of heritage and served as a reminder of the power of food to bring people together.
“Festive gatherings like this are more than just celebrations—they’re moments that strengthen our bonds as a team,” said a spokesperson from IAQ. “It’s our way of showing appreciation to our employees and celebrating the cultural richness that exists within IAQ.”
As the event drew to a close, the message was clear: IAQ values both excellence in work and the importance of shared experiences. The company extends its warmest wishes to all partners, clients, and community members—Selamat Hari Raya Aidilfitri, Maaf Zahir dan Batin. May this season be filled with joy, peace, and meaningful connections.
