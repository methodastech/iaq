Supporting Compassion and Community at the Charity Luncheon for House of Love
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 master_admin 
 / April 10, 2025 
At IAQ, we are dedicated to making a difference both within our industry and in the communities around us. Recently, our team had the privilege of participating in a recognition and charity luncheon for House of Love, an organization committed to providing shelter, support, and hope to children in need. 
This event is a meaningful occasion to celebrate the strength of our community and recognize the inspiring work of those who uplift and support vulnerable children. The luncheon highlighted the impact we can create together, reminding us of all of the importance of compassion and collaboration. 
We are proud to stand with House of Love in their invaluable mission and look forward to future opportunities to support causes that matter. Together, we are building a brighter, kinder world. ????
