IAQ Named Builder of the Year at MCIEA 2024: Setting New Standards in High-Tech Facility Construction
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / November 25, 2024 
IAQ is proud of the achievement in our company’s journey—we have been named Builder of the Year at the Malaysian Construction Industry Excellence Awards (MCIEA) 2024, organized by CIDB Malaysia.
This national honor is a powerful endorsement of our dedication to excellence, precision, and innovation in the construction of high-tech facilities, including cleanrooms, wafer fabrication plants, and data centers. It also serves as recognition of the strides we continue to make in supporting Malaysia’s rise as a leader in advanced industrial infrastructure.
Honored by Industry Peers
We are especially grateful to CIDB Malaysia not only for this prestigious award, but also for the insightful feature article, “Setting New Standards in Construction: Builder of the Year Award 2024.” The piece reflects our unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and safety in everything we build.
Advancing the Future of High-Tech Construction
At IAQ, we are committed to pushing the boundaries of construction technology. Whether delivering clean environments for semiconductor manufacturing or designing critical infrastructure for data-driven industries, our focus is always on sustainability, precision, and safety.
This award is more than just a recognition of past achievements—it is a motivation to continue raising the bar, setting new benchmarks for excellence in engineering and construction, and contributing to Malaysia’s growing reputation as a hub for high-tech development.
We thank CIDB Malaysia for this incredible honor and for continuously promoting innovation and excellence in the construction industry.
