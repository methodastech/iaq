IAQ Sets a New Safety Benchmark in Sarawak: Recognized by DOSH Kuching
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / July 3, 2025 
We are proud to announce another significant Environmental, Health & Safety (EHS) milestone achieved on our Sarawak project.
Our team recently presented this accomplishment to the Department of Occupational Safety and Health (DOSH) Kuching, Sarawak, a formal recognition of the rigorous safety standards we uphold on every project we undertake.
What makes this milestone particularly meaningful is the acknowledgement it received: the Director of JKKP commended IAQ’s safety policies and standards, noting that our approach sets a distinctly higher bar compared to prevailing local contractor practices.
This recognition is a direct reflection of our people. Every member of the Sarawak project team has demonstrated an unwavering commitment to EHS excellence showing up each day with the diligence and discipline that safety demands. This achievement belongs to them.
At IAQ, safety is not a compliance checkbox. It is the foundation upon which we deliver world-class semiconductor manufacturing facilities and this milestone is a testament to that belief.
We remain committed to raising the standard, project by project.
