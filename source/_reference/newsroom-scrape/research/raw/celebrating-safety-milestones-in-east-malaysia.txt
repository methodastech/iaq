Celebrating Safety Milestones in East Malaysia
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / December 14, 2024 
IAQ proudly celebrated a major safety milestone achieved by our project team in East Malaysia. This celebration recognized the team’s dedication to maintaining a safe working environment throughout the project’s duration.
Our East Malaysia project site has achieved an outstanding 2,000,000 safety manhours without a lost-time injury (LTI). This remarkable accomplishment reflects the entire team’s hard work and unwavering focus on safety protocols.
At IAQ, workplace safety is not just a priority; it’s a core value. We firmly believe that every team member deserves to work in a safe and secure environment. We are committed to continuously improving our safety practices and fostering a culture where safety always comes first.
These achievements are a direct result of our commitment to our core values as we strive to become a great company. We are incredibly grateful to every team member and project stakeholder who contributed to this success. During the celebrations, we took the opportunity to express our sincere appreciation for their dedication and hard work.
In summary, IAQ’s recent celebration in East Malaysia highlights our unwavering commitment to workplace safety and the remarkable achievement of 2,000,000 safe manhours without a lost-time injury. This success is a testament to our team’s dedication and our core values as we strive for excellence.
