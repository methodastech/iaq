Spreading Love & Joy This Chinese New Year
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 master_admin 
 / November 5, 2024 
As we step into 2025, we begin the year with a meaningful act—giving back to society. In the spirit of Chinese New Year, IAQ Solutions had the privilege of sharing love and joy with the children at House of Love, many of whom have faced abandonment, abuse, and neglect. 
Over time, our bond with these children has grown stronger, and we remain committed to supporting them again this year because every child deserves warmth, happiness, and a reason to smile—especially during festive celebrations. 
Through this initiative, we hope to inspire our team to actively participate in more charity events, embodying the powerful message: 
“We are the ones who make a brighter day, so let’s start giving.”
