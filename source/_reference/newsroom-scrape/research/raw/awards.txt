List of Awards and Recognition 
AUG 22 
1 million safety manhour for Robert Bosch Project 
IAQ is pleased to share that we have achieved 1 million safety manhour milestone on Robert Bosch project.
As a total facility solution provide who prides in our quality and engineering excellence, safety is at the top of our priority. 
DEC 22 
Recognition from CIDB for Compliance towards Act 520 (LEVI) 
IAQ is honored and happy to receive the token appreciation from CIDB for compliance towards Act 520 (LEVI) from CIDB.
In IAQ, we pride ourselves in the quality work we delivered, in compliance and in accordance to the law, rules and regulation.
We would like to thank CIDB for the token of appreciation and is keen to serve our clients. 
MAY 24 
1 million safety manhours for XFAB, Kuching Project 
Congratulations to our dedicated team in East Malaysia for achieving an impressive milestone – a 1,000,000 safety man-hours without a single lost injury! This achievement is proof of our commitment to safety and the result of a collaborative effort with all project stakeholders.
This achievement has proven that safety is a priority and a core value embedded in our work culture. A safe workplace is essential. 
DEC 23 
500,000 safety manhours for XFAB, Kuching Project 
IAQ is excited to announce another significant achievement at IAQ – 500,000 safety manhours without lost injury for our project in East Malaysia, in collaboration with other project stakeholders.
At IAQ, safety is paramount. This achievement reflects our steadfast commitment to prioritize safety first as we stride towards becoming a Total Facility Solutions Provider. 
JUL 23 
5 Million safety manhours milestone for Infineon, Kulim Project 
 Few media to celebrate achieving 5 million safety manhour milestone on Infineon project with other key project stakeholders. 
As a total facility solution provide who prides in our quality and engineering excellence, safety is at the top of our priority.
Congratulation to the team for the achievement! 
JUL 24 
Appreciation Certificate by Infineon Technologies (Kulim) Sdn. Bhd. - National OSH Award for Year 2023 
 We are proud to share that our client has awarded an appreciation certificate to IAQ in recognition of our collaborative efforts and excellence. Let’s make this week impactful and engaging for everyone involved. 
OCT 24 
Gold Award for OSH Management in Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Maintenance Services 
 We are honoured to receive the Gold Award for OSH Management in Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Maintenance Services at the 20th OSH Excellence Awards 2024, organized by MOSHPA. This prestigious recognition is a testament to our unwavering dedication to enforcing safety standards across all our projects.
👏 A big congratulations to our outstanding safety team for their hard work and diligence, ensuring every IAQ project is in full compliance with Malaysia’s safety regulations. Their commitment and relentless efforts during audits have earned this incredible achievement.
A heartfelt thank you to all our staff for continuously upholding a culture of safety and health in the workplace. Your dedication makes IAQ a safer, healthier place for everyone, and we look forward to carrying this commitment forward in every project and beyond. 
NOV 24 
MCIEA 2024 Award - Builder of the year category G7 
 We at IAQ Solutions Sdn Bhd are deeply honoured to be named the Builder of the Year for MCIEA Award 2024!
At IAQ Solutions, we are committed to pushing boundaries in high-tech facility construction—be it cleanrooms, wafer fabrication facilities or data centres. Our focus remains on innovation, precision, and sustainable practices to meet the ever-evolving needs of the industry while ensuring the highest safety standards.
This award is more than just a milestone for IAQ, it is also a motivator for us to continue setting benchmarks and contributing to Malaysia’s reputation as a hub for cutting-edge facility construction.”
