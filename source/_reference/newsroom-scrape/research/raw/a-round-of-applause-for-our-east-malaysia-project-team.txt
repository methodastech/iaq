A Round of Applause for Our East Malaysia Project Team!
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / May 8, 2024 
We are thrilled to announce a remarkable achievement by our devoted team in East Malaysia. They have reached an extraordinary milestone of 1,000,000 safety man-hours without a single injury lost. This accomplishment is not just a number, but a testament to our unwavering commitment to safety and the synergistic collaboration with all project stakeholders.
This milestone serves as a powerful reminder that safety isn’t just a priority, but a deeply ingrained core value in our work culture. It underscores our belief that a safe workplace isn’t just a requirement, but a fundamental necessity.
So, let’s raise a toast to our team’s relentless dedication and tireless efforts. Let’s look forward to celebrating many more such milestones and achievements in the future! Keep soaring high, team! Your hard work and commitment are truly appreciated.
