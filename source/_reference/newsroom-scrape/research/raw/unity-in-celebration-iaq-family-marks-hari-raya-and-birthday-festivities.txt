Unity in Celebration: IAQ Family Marks Hari Raya and Birthday Festivities
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / May 15, 2024 
Just last week, our IAQ family gathered in unison to commemorate the dual joyous occasions of Hari Raya and a birthday celebration right at our office. It was a lively festivity brimming with mouth-watering delicacies, eye-catching Hari Raya decorations, and our committed team members showcasing their festive Raya attire in full spirit.
Following a month of dedicated fasting and observing holidays, we were overjoyed to finally orchestrate our eagerly-awaited Raya celebrations within the office premises. Each and every member of the IAQ team graced the event, contributing to the warmth and camaraderie that filled the air.
This event was more than just a celebration – it was a testament to our unity, resilience, and collective happiness. We eagerly anticipate many more such occasions that strengthen our bonds and foster a sense of belonging within our team. Here’s to unity, joy, and shared success!
