Spreading Love and Joy This Chinese New Year: IAQ Reaffirms Its Commitment to Community
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / February 19, 2025 
As the new year unfolds, IAQ began 2025 with a gesture close to the heart—by giving back to the community. In celebration of Chinese New Year, our team visited the children at House of Love, continuing a tradition of compassion, empathy, and support for those in need.
House of Love is a sanctuary for children who have experienced abandonment, abuse, or neglect. Over time, our connection with the children has deepened, and this year, we were once again honoured to bring festive cheer to their lives. Our visit was filled with heartfelt moments, gifts, laughter, and meaningful interactions that captured the true spirit of the season.
At IAQ, we believe that corporate success must be matched with social responsibility. Every child deserves to feel joy, love, and the warmth of a caring community—especially during meaningful occasions like Chinese New Year.
More than a seasonal outreach, this initiative reflects our larger commitment to building not only facilities, but a culture of compassion. Through small acts of kindness, we strive to create a lasting impact, inspiring our team to engage in more charitable causes and to live by the words:
“We are the ones who make a brighter day, so let’s start giving.”
Together, let us continue spreading light, hope, and happiness—one act of kindness at a time.
