IAQ Partners with SJKC Choong Hua: Elevating Student Learning Experience
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / March 20, 2024 
IAQ is thrilled to share our recent collaboration with SJKC Choong Hua, a testament to our steadfast dedication to community well-being. Our donation is geared towards enriching the learning environment and ensuring students thrive.
Enhancing School Hall Infrastructure: Recognizing the importance of comfort, especially amidst sweltering temperatures, we’ve allocated resources to upgrade the school’s hall infrastructure for better heat mitigation. Our goal is to create a conducive environment where students can concentrate on their studies comfortably.
We extend an invitation to all our employees to join us in championing meaningful causes. Whether through volunteering or financial support, together, we can drive positive change within our community.
Let’s continue to give back and leave a lasting impact!
