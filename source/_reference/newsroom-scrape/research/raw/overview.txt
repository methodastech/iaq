About IAQ 
IAQ At a glance 
Founded in 1994, the IAQ Group is a Malaysia-based leading Total Facility Solutions Provider with over 30 years of technical excellence in Hi-Tech facility solutions through various service models. 
IAQ’s expertise in facility solutions covers various industries, such as semiconductors, district cooling and heating plant and life science to industrial infrastructure, Oil and Gas, petrochemical as well as power plant. IAQ offers a broad range of services and provides comprehensive turnkey solutions with the highest quality and safety standards to technical-demanding clients.  
32 years of technical excellence through various service model with global presence across 8 countries 
IAQ is a leading ISO9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certified Malaysian owned company that delivers engineering design services, procurement services, construction, commissioning & maintenance services of plants, and on-board project management services to client in diverse industries both locally and globally.  
IAQ Vision 
To be regional facility solutions provider with engineering excellence to facilitate technological innovation and advancement in quality of life. 
IAQ Mission 
Providing innovative and sustainable facility and engineering solutions benefitting our Clients & Stakeholders, driven by our leadership, employees, and partner globally. 
IAQ Core Values 
Safety Commitment 
IAQ is committed to an injury free workplace where everybody goes home safe. Senior leadership engagement to proactively manage risks during project execution. Active risk management and improvement techniques, mitigate risk to reputation and significant cost through collective safety commitment. Enforcing and upscaling best working practices, safety methodologies through multilingual training and educational aids is at the forefront of everything that we do. 
Quality Consistency 
Our team has the skills to bring Excellence to your project. QMS (Quality Management System) are established to proactively manage quality, such as PQP (Project Quality Plan), procedures, ITPs (Inspection Test Plan), witness hold points, records. Incorporating digitalized quality management software and programme coupled with project management excellence enables single point of contact dynamic throughout the entire construction, ensuring project progression are with absolute quality. 
Honesty and Integrity 
Honesty and integrity serve as the cornerstone of our organizational ethos, defining our core code of conduct. In IAQ, we believe in the transparent communication of accurate information, encouraging openness and sincerity in all interactions. Upholding consistency between words and actions, we prioritize ethical practices and moral principles, fostering a culture of trust among our team members and stakeholders. 
Engineering Capabilities 
Our experienced multi-disciplinary in-house Engineering team is capable of integrating technology and value engineering solutions for advanced hi technology facilities. With over 20 years of excellence, our multi-rounded solutions and considerations are tailored, providing total facility solutions to our esteemed clients. 
Efficiency & Proficiency 
Flexible Fast-Track Execution and Contractual approaches are available to fit Client’s needs. IAQ is driven with innovative engineering solutions to accelerate build and time to market. Construction timeline is tracked by multiple critical paths to ensure project progression align with schedule and resources planned. IAQ is committed to maximizes available resources to achieve the best result collectively. 
Pursuit of Excellence 
IAQ will deliver on our commitments with highest degree of engineering excellence. Our customer-focused approach to project execution fosters mutual trust and credibility, conducting business in accordance with the highest Ethical, Moral and Legal standards.
