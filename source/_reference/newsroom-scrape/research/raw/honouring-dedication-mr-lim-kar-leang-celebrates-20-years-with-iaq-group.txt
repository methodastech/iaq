Introducing Our #EmployeeInSpotlight: Mr. Lim Kar Leang
We are immensely proud to honor Mr. Lim Kar Leang, our Senior Project Executive, for his two decades of dedicated service to IAQ Group. At this year’s Annual Dinner, we celebrated Mr. Lim’s remarkable contributions by presenting him with a prestigious long-service award.
Mr. Lim began his illustrious career with IAQ Group in 2004 as an M&E Site Supervisor. His exceptional skills and unwavering commitment led to his secondment to IAQ Morocco in 2009 for a significant project. Upon his return to Malaysia in 2011, he was promoted to Project Executive, reflecting his outstanding performance and dedication. A decade later, his continuous excellence earned him another well-deserved promotion to Senior Project Executive. He is currently making a substantial impact on one of our Mega Projects in Kulim, Kedah.
Our token of appreciation is a small gesture compared to Mr. Lim’s extraordinary dedication and loyalty to IAQ Group. We look forward to many more years of collaboration and wish Mr. Lim continued success in his journey with IAQ.
