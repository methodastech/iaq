IAQ Named Builder of the Year at MCIEA 2024
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / November 19, 2024 
IAQ is proud to announce a defining moment in our company’s journey—we have been honored with the prestigious “Builder of the Year” award by the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) Malaysia at the Malaysian Construction Industry Excellence Awards (MCIEA) 2024.
This national recognition affirms our unwavering dedication to quality, innovation, and delivery excellence within Malaysia’s construction sector, especially in the realm of high-tech facility development. Receiving this honour is not just a milestone for IAQ—it is a celebration of the collaborative effort, trust, and resilience that define our work.
A Project That Defined Our Commitment
This award would not have been possible without the trust placed in us by our valued client, Robert Bosch, who appointed IAQ as the General Contractor for a transformative project in 2020. Despite the complex challenges brought on by the COVID-19 pandemic, our team remained steadfast in our mission—delivering the project on time and upholding the highest construction standards.
Collaborative Excellence
We extend our sincere gratitude to M.E.I for their technical expertise and strategic guidance throughout the project. Their partnership was instrumental in navigating complexities and ensuring successful execution.
Equally, we thank our dedicated subcontractors, whose craftsmanship and cooperation were vital to achieving this outcome. Your professionalism continues to be the foundation of IAQ’s success, and we look forward to building more milestones together.
Raising the Bar in the Industry
This recognition reinforces IAQ’s role as a Total Facility Solutions Provider for high-tech industries—from cleanrooms to mission-critical infrastructure. It also symbolizes the collective spirit of our teams, clients, and partners who believe in our vision and values.
We remain committed to pushing boundaries, delivering excellence, and shaping the future of Malaysia’s construction landscape. Here’s to more partnerships, innovation, and milestones ahead.
