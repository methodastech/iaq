Bridging the Gap: IAQ Shares Real-World Engineering Insight at University of Malaya
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / June 9, 2025 
IAQ recently had the privilege of conducting a knowledge-sharing session at the University of Malaya through a dynamic workshop titled “Engineering Realities and Career Insight: Beyond Blueprint.” This event marked another meaningful step in our ongoing commitment to nurturing the next generation of engineers.
From Theory to Practice: What It Really Takes
Led by IAQ’s seasoned professional engineers, the session was designed to go beyond the textbook. It offered students a candid look at the realities of working in the engineering and construction industry—from managing high-pressure project timelines and overcoming on-site challenges to collaborating across disciplines on complex builds such as cleanrooms and high-tech industrial facilities.
“Engineering is more than just drawing or calculations. It’s about problem-solving under pressure, communication, safety, and delivering real results that matter.”
Through real-life case studies and personal experiences, our team illustrated the day-to-day demands and rewards of an engineering career, while highlighting the human, technical, and strategic elements that define project success in the field.
Shaping Tomorrow’s Engineers
By creating a platform for dialogue, mentorship, and experiential learning, IAQ aims to empower students with the knowledge and mindset to thrive in their future careers. Workshops like these are crucial in helping students connect their academic foundation with the evolving demands of the industry.
A Thank You to University of Malaya
We are grateful to the University of Malaya for the opportunity to engage with such promising talent and for fostering an environment where academic excellence meets industry relevance.
To the students who joined us—thank you for your enthusiasm and curiosity. Your journey as engineers is just beginning, and we encourage you to always think beyond the blueprint.
