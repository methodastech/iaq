Inspiring Future Engineers: IAQ at UMENGINE Career Day 2025
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / April 28, 2025 
IAQ proudly returned to Universiti Malaya for UMENGINE Career Day 2025, reaffirming our commitment to nurturing the next generation of engineering professionals. As part of our continuous engagement with future talents, the event served as an excellent platform to connect with students and share a real-world perspective on engineering in the construction industry.
As returning participants, the IAQ team was delighted to showcase not only our portfolio of work in high-tech facility construction but also the values, culture, and career opportunities that define our company. From site-level technical challenges to cross-disciplinary teamwork, we shared insights into the dynamic and rewarding journey of engineers at IAQ—where innovation meets impact.
“Engineers are not just problem-solvers—they are visionaries who shape the future,” said Ir. Then Kim Kian, Assistant Electrical Manager at IAQ, during his keynote address. His speech captivated the audience with firsthand accounts of engineering challenges and triumphs, offering students a glimpse into what it takes to thrive in the industry.
At IAQ, we believe in more than building infrastructure—we build careers, cultivate character, and champion a culture of collaboration and ethical leadership. Career fairs such as UMENGINE are vital to bridging the gap between academia and industry, and we are proud to contribute to the professional development of Malaysia’s brightest young minds.
To the students we met: thank you for your curiosity, questions, and enthusiasm. We look forward to seeing many of you shape the future of engineering—and we hope some of those journeys begin with IAQ.
Together, we engineer possibilities.
