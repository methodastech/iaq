Celebrating 2.6 million Safe Manhours!
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 master_admin 
 / April 10, 2025 
The XFAB 40K Expansion Project is pleased to announce their achievement in reaching 2.6 million safe manhours since the inception of the project. We crossed this successful milestone on the 28th of February 2025. 
Despite the many challenges faced through the lifespan of the project, our team remained steadfast in ensuring a safe working environment, by implementing a robust project EHS program, proactive safety measures, and risk assessment processes. This contributed largely to our zero lost time injury (LTI) record. 
This achievement could not have occurred without the leadership and commitment of the IAQ management, staff, and workers and our Subcontractors. This was truly a team effort, and we thank everyone involved.
