Celebrating Unity and Togetherness at IAQ’s Cross-Department Iftar Gathering
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / March 28, 2025 
In the spirit of Ramadan, IAQ recently hosted a cross-department Iftar gathering that brought team members from across our headquarters together for a meaningful evening of reflection, connection, and community.
Held at the elegant M Resort & Hotel, the event was especially significant for our Muslim colleagues observing the holy month, providing a moment to break fast in the company of fellow team members. More than just a shared meal, the evening served as an opportunity to unwind, engage in conversation, and strengthen the bonds that extend beyond day-to-day collaboration.
This gathering reflects IAQ’s commitment to fostering a people-first culture—one that values inclusivity, respect, and genuine human connection as the foundation of our workplace. By bringing different departments together in an informal setting, we reinforce the spirit of unity that drives our collective success.
At IAQ, we believe that building strong relationships among our people is just as essential as building high-tech facilities. Moments like these remind us of the power of shared experiences in shaping a supportive and dynamic work environment.
