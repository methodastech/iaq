Quality, Environment, Health and Safety 
The IAQ Group is committed to Environment, Safety and Health (EHS) as a core business function, promoting safe and healthy working conditions without risk of harm to our employees or others that may be affected by our undertakings and/or harm to assets and the environment. 
 We have established the highest levels of EHS standards and apply the concept
of proactive EHS management through responsible planning and implementation.
We firmly believe that all incidents are preventable and that there is no task so
important that is worth endangering the health & wellbeing of our employees and
partners.

 We possess an effective EHS Management System compliant with local legislation, client requirements, and international standards ISO 9001, 14001 & 45001. 
ISO 9001: Quality Management System
IAQ Solutions is proud to be ISO 9001 certified, which signifies that our quality management system (QMS) aligns with globally recognized standards. This certification ensures that we consistently deliver high-quality products and services by following efficient processes and maintaining a strong focus on customer satisfaction. ISO 9001 drives us to continually improve our operations, reduce errors, and optimize performance, ensuring that we meet the needs and expectations of our clients in every project. 
ISO 14001: Environmental Management System
At IAQ Solutions, we are committed to sustainable practices, as demonstrated by our ISO 14001 certification. This certification establishes that we have an effective environmental management system (EMS) in place. It enables us to systematically manage and reduce our environmental impact across all aspects of our operations. From minimizing waste and conserving natural resources to lowering energy consumption and carbon emissions, we strive to operate in an environmentally responsible manner. ISO 14001 reflects our dedication to sustainability and environmental stewardship in the industries we serve. 
ISO 45001: Occupational Health and Safety Management System
Safety is a top priority at IAQ Solutions, and our ISO 45001 certification is a testament to that commitment. This international standard for occupational health and safety (OHS) ensures that we provide a safe and healthy working environment for all employees, contractors, and visitors. By complying with ISO 45001, we systematically identify and manage workplace hazards, prevent accidents, and promote a culture of safety. We continuously assess and improve our safety practices to protect our workforce and ensure compliance with all relevant regulations.
