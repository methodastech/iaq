IAQ Brings Joy to Children from House of Love with a Day at Chocolate Museum Malaysia
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / August 13, 2025 
As part of IAQ’s ongoing commitment to community engagement, our team had the privilege of spending a day with the children of House of Love at Chocolate Museum Malaysia, an afternoon dedicated entirely to exploration, creativity, and the kind of uncomplicated joy that stays with you long after the day is done.
From discovering the museum’s exhibits to crafting their own chocolate creations, the children were fully immersed in an experience designed to spark curiosity and delight. For our team, witnessing that sense of wonder, the wide eyes, the laughter, the quiet concentration of a child at work, was a powerful reminder of why moments like these matter.
At IAQ, we believe that every child deserves the opportunity to experience the world beyond their immediate surroundings to learn new things, to create with their hands, and to simply enjoy being a child. Community initiatives such as this one are not ancillary to what we do; they are an expression of the values that define us as an organization.
We are grateful to House of Love for welcoming us into their community, and to our IAQ team members who gave their time and energy to make the day a meaningful one. We hope the memories made that afternoon will stay with these children for years to come.
