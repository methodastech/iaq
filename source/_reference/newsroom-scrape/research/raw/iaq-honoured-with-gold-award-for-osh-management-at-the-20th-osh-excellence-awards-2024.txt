IAQ Honoured with Gold Award for OSH Management at the 20th OSH Excellence Awards 2024
 Leave a Comment 
 / By 
 Nabilahhradzi 
 / October 24, 2024 
IAQ is proud to celebrate a significant milestone in our journey toward operational excellence and workplace safety. We are honoured to have received the Gold Award for Occupational Safety and Health (OSH) Management in the Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Maintenance Services category at the 20th OSH Excellence Awards 2024, organised by the Malaysian Occupational Safety and Health Practitioners’ Association (MOSHPA).
This prestigious recognition reflects our steadfast commitment to upholding the highest safety standards across all our projects and operations. At IAQ, safety is more than a compliance measure—it is a core value that informs every decision, procedure, and practice across the company.
We extend our heartfelt congratulations to our exceptional safety team, whose unwavering diligence, especially during rigorous audits, has been instrumental in achieving this award. Their expertise and commitment ensure that every IAQ site not only meets but exceeds Malaysia’s stringent safety regulations.
We also thank all IAQ staff members for consistently nurturing a strong safety culture, where health and well-being remain central to our working environment. It is through collective effort that we maintain a workplace where every individual feels secure, respected, and empowered.
This award serves as both a proud achievement and a reminder of the responsibility we carry. As we continue to grow, we remain dedicated to raising the bar for safety and health excellence in the engineering and construction sectors—today and into the future.
