Marks: art and motion, the six services (25 Sep 2026)

Six more drawn scenes on the same rules as the value marks above, so the services have their own set and the site has variety: the dotted drafting ground, ink line work at 1.6, white and one pale grey, the plain number top right, and one movement in IAQ red that says what the service does. Every red movement runs horizontally or turns, never down a vertical line. Each loop runs about six seconds, a beat apart, only while it is on screen; under reduced motion the finished drawing is the still.
