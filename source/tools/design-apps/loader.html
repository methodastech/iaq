Loading screen

The first thing every visitor sees: the dotted world on white, the IAQ mark filling red as the page loads. Four ways to run it, each looping here at the speed it plays on the site. Number 4 is approved and live.

The live one, checked on 26 Sep. All dots, no sphere behind them, 60 frames a second; on a first visit it reaches 100% at 2.4 s and lifts at 2.9 s on a phone, 2.8 s and 3.3 s on a laptop (measured on the development server). From the first frame the page paints the loader’s white and the pale mark, so the page frame never shows before it. Number 4 went live on 26 Sep and was approved the same day; all four keep the mark filling red as the load runs.
