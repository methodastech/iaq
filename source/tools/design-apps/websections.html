Website sections

Nine section types build every page of the site: each is drawn below from the live copy, then set out as how it is built, what to do and what never to do.

✓Do

  • The logo file, never the letters typed, at its own proportion.
  • One red phrase in a head, kept whole on one line, and one button per section.
  • Words beside a picture; on a picture only on a solid panel flush to its top left corner.
  • Rows on space and tint, with one long red line under a head at most.

✕Don’t

  • No mark in a box, tile or disc, and no icon beside every row.
  • No line under every row, no vertical rule, no frame line round a picture.
  • No numbers before names, no tracked capitals, no eyebrow over a head.
  • No figure the site does not publish, and no client name or logo.

Each mock is drawn at a 1440 px window and scaled as one picture, so every size below is the size on the site.

Navigation bar

The logo on the left, six links and the action cluster on the right, the page you are on in red. It is the same bar on every page.

How it is built

Bar
76 px tall on the paper ground #F7F9FC. No line under it: the ground is the edge.
Columns
Logo on the left gutter (the site’s 1.5 vw, 22 px at 1440). Links and actions pushed to the right.
Logo
The drawn file, /assets/iaq-logo.webp, 111 × 46 px, kept at its 668 × 276 proportion.
Links
About, Services, Markets, Projects, News, Careers. Switzer 500, 13.5 px, #48536A, 12 px either side.
Actions
The 21 px drawn lens (1.35 stroke) in a 40 px target, 10 px from the button. Button 40 px tall, 18 px sides, Switzer 600, 14 px, square.
Red
The logo, the current page (Switzer 600) and the one button. Nothing else.

✓Do

  • The drawn logo. The file itself at its own proportion, never the letters typed.
  • One red link. The page you are on turns red; the other five stay grey.
  • One button. Start a project is the only filled box in the bar.

✕Don’t

  • A typed wordmark. IAQ set in Switzer is not the logo, at any size.
  • Marks on the links. No dot, underline, pill or box behind the current page.
  • A second button or a rule under the bar. One action, and space for the edge.

Hero

The headline with its one red phrase, one sentence under it and one button, beside a photograph block or the 3D stage. Type never sits on the picture.

How it is built

Columns
Two equal halves, 48 px apart: the words left, the photograph or the 3D stage right.
Spacing
88 px above and below. 28 px between headline, lede and button.
Headline
Switzer 500, 72 px, 1.08 line height, tracked in by 1.8 px. Three lines, the red phrase whole on the last.
Lede
Instrument Sans, 18.5 px, 1.6, #48536A. One sentence, 540 px at most.
Photograph
Square corners, cover crop, 520 px tall, true colour.
Red
The emphasised phrase and the one button.

✓Do

  • Words beside the picture. Headline, lede and button in their half; the photograph in the other.
  • One red phrase, kept whole. It sits on one line and never breaks.
  • One sentence, one button. Start a project, and nothing competing with it.

✕Don’t

  • A headline on the photograph. No scrim and no fade behind words.
  • An eyebrow above the headline or a second button beside the first.
  • Rounded corners or a glow on the picture.

Figures ledger

The site’s published figures and nothing else: the four in the Services page’s record, with its own labels, on one long red line under the head.

How it is built

Figures
Exactly the four the site publishes, STATS in src/pages/ServicesHub.jsx: 250+, 1,050,000, 450, 7.
Columns
Four equal, 24 px apart, on one row.
Spacing
96 px above and below. 56 px head to line, 36 px line to figures, 12 px figure to label.
Type
Figures Switzer 500, 64 px, tabular. Labels Switzer 500, 15 px, #48536A, the site’s own words.
Line
One long red line under the head, 1.5 px, margin to margin. Ground paper #F7F9FC.
Red
The head’s phrase and the line. The figures stay ink.

✓Do

  • Only published figures. The four the site carries, with the site’s labels.
  • Figures on one line. One red line under the head, the figures on space beneath it.
  • The number is the emphasis. Figures in ink, labels quiet.

✕Don’t

  • A new or rounded figure. Nothing the site does not already publish.
  • Cards, icons or vertical rules round or between the figures.
  • Capitals in the labels. Labels stay in sentence case.

Services

The six services in the cycle’s order, each with the drawn mark the live Services page uses, on space. The kind name leads in services red.

How it is built

Head
The kind name, 6 services, Switzer 500, 36 px, services red, the cycle mark at 1 em. The lede, Instrument Sans 19 px, in the right half.
Columns
Six equal, 24 px apart, 64 px under the head. 104 px above, 88 px below.
Marks
The six drawn marks of the live cycle (src/data/cycleMarks.js), each fitted to the same visual weight, 120 px square, on space.
Type
Name Switzer 600, 18 px, 20 px under the mark. One line in Instrument Sans 15 px, #48536A.
Order
The cycle, Design to Tools Hookup. The order carries the sequence; no numbers.
Red
The kind name. Each mark carries its own red part.

✓Do

  • The drawn marks at one weight. All six from the live cycle, 120 px, fitted to each other.
  • Marks on space. The section’s own ground is the only thing behind a mark.
  • Name, then one line. The service’s name and what it does.

✕Don’t

  • A mark in a box, tile or disc.
  • Numbers before the names. The cycle’s order already carries the sequence.
  • A second icon set on the same row, or a mark redrawn at another weight.

Split section

Text beside a photograph block with square corners. Words sit on the photograph only on a solid white panel, flush to its top left corner.

How it is built

Columns
5 to 7, 64 px apart. Text in the narrow column, the photograph in the wide one. Sides may alternate down a page.
Photograph
Square corners, cover crop, 560 px tall, true colour, no filter.
On the picture
Only a solid panel: white, square, flush to the top left corner with no gap, 18 by 22 px inside, Switzer 600 16 px ink. A field red panel with white type is the one alternative.
Type
Head Switzer 500, 50 px. Lede Instrument Sans 18.5 px. Link Switzer 600, 15 px, the drawn arrow 18 px, 10 px after the words.
Red
The head’s phrase. The panel is white.

✓Do

  • Words beside the picture. Head, lede and one link in their own column.
  • A solid panel for words on a picture. White or field red, square, flush to the top left corner.
  • Square corners, true colour. The photograph as it was taken, cropped clean.

✕Don’t

  • Type straight on the photograph, with or without a shadow.
  • A fade, scrim or colour filter over the picture in a split section.
  • Rounded corners or a frame line round the picture.

Markets band

The seven markets in the site’s order, in the markets violet: the head’s phrase and the seven marks. There is no red in the band.

How it is built

Colour
Markets violet #6E56E6 (#9D8CF5 on dark) for the head’s phrase and the seven marks.
Columns
Head and lede in two halves. The seven markets in seven equal columns, 16 px apart, 56 px under the head.
Each market
Photograph at 3 by 4, square corners. The market mark 32 px, 18 px under it on the text edge. Name Switzer 600, 17 px. One line in Instrument Sans 14 px.
Marks
The site’s market marks (HeroLineMarks, 24 grid, 0.95 stroke, butt ends).
Spacing
96 px above and below. Ground paper #F7F9FC.
Red
None. Violet carries the band.

✓Do

  • Violet for markets. The phrase and the marks, and nothing else in colour.
  • All seven, in the site’s order, each with photograph, mark, name and one line.
  • The link as words with the drawn arrow after them.

✕Don’t

  • Red in the band. Red belongs to services and the button.
  • Tiles or discs behind the marks, or names set on the photographs.
  • Vertical rules between the columns. Space separates them.

Project record rows

The registry as rows: photograph, project and client sector, location, class and market, one arrow. These are the five records the Services page proves with.

How it is built

Columns
Photograph 120 px, project 1fr, location 200 px, class 150 px, market 230 px, arrow 20 px. 24 px apart.
Rows
16 px inside, held apart by alternate paper tint #F3F5F8, never a line each. The column names in Switzer 500 13 px, #66708A.
Type
Project Switzer 600, 17 px, the client sector under it in Instrument Sans 14 px. Location and class Switzer 500 15 px. Market Switzer 500 in violet.
Arrow
The drawn arrow, 20 px, centred on the row, the same on every row.
Records
From src/data/projects.js, the five the Services page proves with. Clients by sector only.
Red
The head’s phrase and the one long line under the head.

✓Do

  • Real records only. Name, client sector, location, class and market, as the registry holds them.
  • Rows on space and tint. One red line under the head, then alternate tint.
  • One arrow per row, the same size, on the row’s centre.

✕Don’t

  • A line under every row or a vertical rule between columns.
  • Client names or logos. IAQ’s clients are named by sector only.
  • Index numbers before the projects.

Closing band

The dark band that ends every page: the ask and its one button, then the contact rows with the values from the Contact page.

How it is built

Ground
The dark band #0A101F, 112 px above and below.
Columns
Two halves, 64 px apart: the ask left, the contact rows right.
Type
Head Switzer 500, 58 px, white. Lede Instrument Sans 18.5 px, #B7C2D6. Row label Switzer 500 13 px, #8A96AE. Row value Switzer 600 25 px, white.
Contacts
From src/pages/Contact.jsx: business@iaqtechnology.com.my, +603 5124 8319, the Shah Alam headquarters. Rows 28 px apart, no lines.
Arrows
The drawn arrow, 20 px, white, one size in the button and on every row, on the centre of the value’s first line.
Red
The head’s phrase and the one button.

✓Do

  • The ask leads. Head, one line and one button, before any contact detail.
  • The real values. The email and phone exactly as the Contact page carries them.
  • Arrows of one size, each on the first line of its value.

✕Don’t

  • A line between the rows. 28 px of space holds them apart.
  • Capitals on the labels. Email, Phone and Headquarters in sentence case.
  • A glow or a gradient behind the ask.

Footer

The site map on the same dark ground: the logo and descriptor, two columns and Follow, the badges, then the legal foot under one long red line.

How it is built

Ground
The same dark band, #0A101F, 72 px above, 40 px below.
Columns
Brand 2 parts, then The company, What we do and Follow at 1 part each, 40 px apart.
Logo
The drawn file, /design/iaq-official.png, 44 px tall, red on the dark ground.
Type
Heads Switzer 600 14 px white, sentence case. Links Switzer 500 15 px, #AAB6CE. Legal Switzer 500 13 px, #8A96AE, broken into lines.
Badges
CIDB, ISO, UKAS and Highwire, the reversed files, 56 px tall, 28 px apart, no box.
Red
The logo and one long red line above the legal foot. Arrows 16 px, white.

✓Do

  • The drawn logo in red above the descriptor, on the dark ground.
  • Sentence case heads and plain links, one per line.
  • One long red line above the legal foot, and the legal text broken into lines.

✕Don’t

  • Middle dots or slashes between the legal facts.
  • Tracked capitals in the heads or the legal line.
  • A box round the badges or a second line.
