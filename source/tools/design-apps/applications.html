Applications

Eight pieces, drawn in this system and shown as they would be made: acrylic on a wall, a signature in a mail window, covers on a desk, cards on stock, roll ups on a floor, banners in a page, stationery, and signage on site. Six keep the content of IAQ’s drafts; two more set the direction for the red led pieces. Each piece is marked with its colour mode, and what IAQ still has to confirm is written under it.
