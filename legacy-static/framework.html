01
The Flow
Three visitors, one site, three jobs done.
01 · Who arrives, what must happen

Every page earns one of three outcomes

The site is engineered around its three real audiences. Each has a distinct path, and every section exists to move one of them forward. Nothing decorative survives this rule.

Visitor 1 · The buyer

MNC capex & facility leads

Path: lands on Home or a project page from a referral or search, feels the engineering grade in five seconds, filters the registry to their industry and region, forwards two projects to a colleague, submits an enquiry.

Visitor 2 · The investor

Analysts, media, the listing

Path: verifies the story pre-listing: scale, record, governance signals, newsroom. On listing day the IR gate opens and the same visitor finds reports and announcements in two clicks.

Visitor 3 · The hire

Senior engineering talent

Path: checks whether IAQ is career-grade: the projects say yes, the culture signals say yes, the career engine filters to their location and discipline, they apply.

Diagram · the conversion flow, per visitor
The rule that governs everything: five seconds to credibility, thirty seconds to relevant proof, two minutes to action. Every design and engineering choice below serves those three clocks.

Global elements

On every page

Sticky nav with one CTA ("Start a project"), the Investors item visible but lock-marked pre-listing, a compliance footer (ISO 9001, 14001, 45001, CIDB), and the tag spine underneath everything.

The IR gate

How hidden works

The IR wing ships noindex, unlinked from public nav flows, and password-protected at the edge. Content is prepared quietly during development. On listing day the gate opens with one configuration change, no rebuild, no downtime.

05 · The 3D plan

3D that proves the craft, without the weight

3D is the client’s stated priority and the site’s signature. The rule that keeps it premium: every 3D moment demonstrates what IAQ engineers, air, environments, precision, and loads only when it earns its bytes.

Moment 1 · Hero

The laminar field

Turbulent particles resolve into clean laminar flow around the headline. The business, as physics, in the first five seconds. Canvas-based, feather-light.

Moment 2 · Showpiece

The facility, assembled

A dark-band cleanroom cutaway that builds on scroll: shell, MEP, FFU ceiling, laminar downflow. Upgraded to a full WebGL model in production.

Moment 3 · 360° captures

Real sites, explorable

IAQ’s existing 360° camera assets embedded on project pages and the showpiece, placed at decision points rather than cluttering the homepage, exactly as discussed.

Moment 4 · Micro-motion

Apple-grade restraint

Masked headline reveals, count-ups, hover physics, section wipes. Calm, weighted, never bouncy. Full reduced-motion fallbacks.

The Build
The stack decision the performance budget forces.
06 · Stack & operations

Custom build, editable where it matters

Platform

Custom, not WordPress

Hand-built front end, no page-builder runtime, no plugin surface to bloat, break or attack. This is what makes the performance contract and Apple-grade motion physically possible.

Content ops

Structured data, easy edits

Projects, roles and articles live as structured tagged data. IAQ’s team edits content and publishes roles without touching design or code.

Delivery

Fast everywhere

CDN-served, image pipeline that resizes and compresses automatically, no raw uploads reaching production, monitored uptime with maintenance cover.

Security posture for the listing: no advertised CMS version, no third-party plugin surface, the IR wing gated at the edge and unindexed, and one owner for DNS and hosting access, locked early so launch is never blocked.
