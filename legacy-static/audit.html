IAQ Group, Website & Digital Audit

A 32-year engineering group that builds the world’s cleanest environments for Bosch, Infineon and Northvolt, heading for a public listing, represented online by a 26-megabyte template site. This audit reads exactly where the credibility leaks, with measurements, and what the new site has to do to be listing-grade.

The verdict, in 60 seconds

A blue-chip delivery record, published at the grade of a local contractor.

IAQ has the substance a capex committee, an investor or a top engineer wants: 32 years, 230+ completed projects, 1,050,000 m² of cleanroom built-up, floors delivered for Robert Bosch, Infineon, Texas Instruments, Northvolt and Siemens, ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001. The website undersells all of it. The homepage weighs 26 MB and loads 152 separate CSS and JS files through WordPress and Elementor, the projects that are the company’s best proof cannot be filtered by region or type, the career page is a flat list, and there is no investor-ready surface for the listing. The gap is not substance. It is presentation and engineering.

The gap

The proof is buried

The move

Rebuild, listing-grade

A custom, fast, 3D-led site with a tagged project registry, a filterable career engine and a gated investor section, live before the listing window.

Background
Who IAQ is, and why the website matters right now.
01 · Company context

A cleanroom specialist that became a regional facility group

IAQ Group (690214-V), founded 1994 and headquartered in Shah Alam, grew from indoor air quality and HVAC into a total facility solutions provider: engineering design, procurement, construction, testing and commissioning, and maintenance, across semiconductors, EV battery, data centres, photovoltaics, pharma, F&B and district cooling. It runs 450 employees across 8 countries, holds ISO 9001:2015, 14001:2015 and 45001:2018, and has delivered 230+ projects and 1,050,000 m² of cleanroom built-up for clients including Robert Bosch, Infineon, Texas Instruments, MEMC, Silterra, Northvolt, ACC, Svolt, Pharmaniaga, NUS, Siemens and KLCC.

The firm

IAQ Group

Total facility solutions: design, build, test, maintain. Cleanrooms are the crown discipline, ISO class 3 to 8.

The scale

Regional, quietly global

450 employees, 8 countries, 32 years. European gigafactory dry rooms already delivered.

The moment

The public listing

A listing raises the audience from procurement teams to investors, regulators and national media. Every surface gets re-inspected, starting with the website.

The trigger, in the client’s own words: the rebrand is driven by the listing, the current logo sits too close to IBM’s mark for comfort in an IPO trademark review, and the current WordPress and Elementor site is slow and bug-prone. The website revamp is not cosmetic. It is pre-listing infrastructure.

03.3 · Content & consistency audit

The homepage disagrees with itself

Because Elementor duplicates sections per breakpoint, the same facts get edited in one place and missed in another. Found on the live homepage, July 2026:

Numbers conflict

230 vs 235 · 7 vs 8

Completed projects read 230 in one variant and 235 in another; global offices read 7 and 8 in parallel sections of the same page. An analyst notices this.

Copy faults

On the primary surface

"Success project delivery relies on strong leadership along coupled with precise execution”, "is align with”, a heading that reads "Complete Projects", and a paragraph beginning mid-word: "dentification”.

Casing drift

Three versions of one line

"We are Total Solutions provider”, "we are total solutions provider” and "We are Total Solutions Provide” all appear, one per duplicated section.

Third-party credits

"Powered by" in the footer

The corporate footer credits the template vendor. On a listing-bound site, the footer belongs to IAQ alone.

03.4 · Findability & UX

The proof exists; nobody can reach it

Projects

Tabs, not a registry

Seven category tabs, no region or type dimension, no search, no permalinks to share a filtered view with a colleague. The client explicitly asked for industry, region and type filtering.

Careers

A flat list

16 live roles in one column. No location filter, no department filter, no search: the exact gap the client asked us to solve for international hiring.

Investor relations

Nothing gated, nothing ready

The listing requires an IR surface that stays hidden until launch day. Neither the structure nor the access control exists.

Working proof: the Projects and Careers pages inside tab 03 are functioning prototypes of the registry and the career engine, built on IAQ’s real published projects and live openings. This is the audit’s recommendation, already running.

04 · The argument

Three audiences are about to inspect IAQ at once

A cleanroom contract is a trust-led, multi-million-ringgit decision made by MNC capex committees. A listing adds investors and media. Growth adds international engineers. All three self-serve credibility from the website before anyone from IAQ is in the room. Today that surface contradicts its own numbers, loads like a brochure from 2015, and hides the strongest proof. Every week it stays up, deals, valuation perception and hires leak to better-presented, lesser builders.
